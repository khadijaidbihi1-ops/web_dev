MEHEK customer care

Shipping Policy

Last updated: 1 August 2026

Order processing

Orders are normally prepared within 1–2 working days. Processing may take longer during collection launches, public holidays or promotional periods.

UK delivery

Standard UK delivery is estimated at 3–5 working days after dispatch. Complimentary standard shipping applies to orders over £100; a delivery charge may apply below this amount.

Delivery addresses

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address. Please contact us immediately if a correction is required.

Delays and tracking

Delivery dates are estimates rather than guarantees. Where tracking is available, details will be provided after dispatch.

Missing or damaged parcels

Please contact us promptly with your order information and photographs where relevant so the issue can be reviewed.
