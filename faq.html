Customer Care

How may we help?

Find answers about orders, delivery, returns and caring for your MEHEK fragrances.

Orders Delivery Returns Products

01

Orders & Payment

Can I change or cancel my order?

Please contact us as soon as possible. We can only amend or cancel an order before it has been processed for dispatch.

How will I know my order has been confirmed?

After checkout, you will receive an order confirmation. A separate dispatch confirmation will follow when your parcel is on its way.

How does the wishlist work?

Select the heart on any product to save it. Once the same product is added to your bag from any page, it is automatically removed from your wishlist.

02

Delivery

How long does UK delivery take?

Standard UK delivery usually arrives within 3–5 working days after dispatch. Times may vary during launches and seasonal periods.

Do you offer complimentary delivery?

Complimentary UK standard delivery is available on orders over £100.

Can I track my order?

When tracking is available, the details will be included in your dispatch confirmation.

03

Returns

Can I return a fragrance?

Unopened and unused products may be returned within 14 days of delivery. They must remain in their original packaging and condition.

What should I do if my order arrives damaged?

Contact our customer care team promptly with your order details and clear photographs of the parcel and product.

How long does a refund take?

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Your bank may require additional processing time.

04

Products & Fragrance Care

Are MEHEK fragrances unisex?

Many MEHEK fragrances are created beyond traditional gender categories. Each product page provides guidance to help you choose.

What size are the perfumes?

MEHEK Eau de Parfum bottles are 100 ml unless the individual product page states otherwise.

How should I store my fragrance?

Keep your bottle upright in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight, heat and humidity.
