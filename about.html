Our story

Scent was our language before MEHEK had a name.

It lived in family homes, familiar rituals, rose-filled gardens, polished wood and spices carried through the air.

MEHEK was created to preserve those memories and give them a new form: intimate, modern and made to remain.

Some places never leave us.

Our journey

Four places that shaped one fragrance house.

MEHEK brings together the places, cultures and memories that formed our identity.

Chapter 01 1993

Morocco & Pakistan

Our story begins in family homes filled with rose, oud, warm spices and familiar rituals. These early memories became the emotional heart of MEHEK.

Chapter 02 2003

Italy

Italy introduced a new appreciation for architecture, craftsmanship and timeless design. It taught us that refinement can be found in every considered detail.

Chapter 03 2018

London

London became the meeting point of heritage and modern creativity. Its contrasts helped shape a fragrance house that feels international without losing its soul.

Chapter 04 2021

MEHEK

The memories of Morocco, Pakistan, Italy and London became one fragrance house. MEHEK was created to turn meaningful places and personal stories into scent.
