Our story

Scent was our language before MEHEK had a name.

It lived in family homes, familiar rituals, rose-filled gardens, polished wood and spices carried through the air.

MEHEK was created to preserve those memories and give them a new form: intimate, modern and made to remain.

Our journey

Four chapters.
One olfactory home.

1993

Morocco & Pakistan

Where our story begins. Homes filled with rose, oud and family rituals shaped our earliest memories.

2003

Italy

A new chapter of refinement: architecture, craftsmanship and the timeless beauty of considered design.

2018

London

A city of contrasts and freedom, where heritage could be reimagined without losing its soul.

2021

MEHEK

Every place we had known became one fragrance house—our memories, shaped into a new beginning.
